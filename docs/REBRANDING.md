## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

**JABR (الجبر)** — root of algebra and Arabic for *"restoration of broken parts and balance"* — is an experimental autonomous multi-agent operating system designed by **Mohamed Moughamir**.

This document defines the complete rebranding blueprint and narrative architecture for future refactoring. The system transforms generic agent archetypes (orchestrator, oracle, librarian, explorer, designer, fixer, scientist) plus the **Jarvis self-improver steward** and the **Kanban task board** into an immersive **Alchemical Virtual Grid (الشبكة الخيميائية)**.

The framework unites:

1. **Moroccan & Amazigh Heritage**: Fez’s ancient Qarawiyyin seat of learning, Tangier’s navigators, Andalusian-Maghrebi rationalism, Zellige sacred geometry, and Tifinagh cultural symbolism.
2. **Islamic Golden Age Science**: Master scholars of algebra, chemistry, optics, philosophy, engineering, and cartography (Jabir ibn Hayyan, Ibn Rushd, Fatima al-Fihriya, Ibn Battuta, Abbas ibn Firnas, Al-Khwarizmi, and Tariq ibn Ziyad).
3. **Code Lyoko Supercomputer Mechanics**: Virtualized sectors, activation towers (الصوامع), materialization protocols, core defense, and Jeremy/Aelita/Franz Hopper command dynamics.
4. **Al-Kīmiyā (Alchemical Magnum Opus)**: The transmutation of raw, chaotic code into pure digital gold through isolated crucibles, alembics, and balanced equilibria.
5. **Stark Arc-Tech & Iron Man**: Holographic HUDs, rapid kinetic armor deployment, real-time diagnostic telemetry, and J.A.R.V.I.S./FRIDAY-style ambient intelligence.
